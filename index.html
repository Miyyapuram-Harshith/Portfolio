About

I build software that keeps *your *device. Fast, private tools that run in the browser, with no server pipeline in between.

I am a B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at SR University and an independent product builder. Over the past two years I have designed, developed and monetized production-ready web platforms under my studio, MultiCode Studio.

My engineering philosophy puts privacy first: client-side execution in the browser, and no unnecessary server data pipelines. Beyond the web, I work on bare-metal C/C++ firmware, edge-native learning platforms and hardware-software diagnostics. Off screen, I am President of the Sangeetic Club at SR University and play acoustic guitar.
